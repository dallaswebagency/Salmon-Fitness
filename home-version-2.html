Buyer questions

Before you ask

Do you offer OEM and private label?

Yes. We take a product from concept through engineering, prototype and pre-production testing, or build to your existing design with your colour and branding.

Can you match our brand colour?

Yes. Our bespoke colour service matches a specific shade across the range. Send a swatch or brand guide with your enquiry and we will confirm what is possible.

Which standards is the equipment tested to?

We work to EN 957-1 and ASTM F2276-10, with static and dynamic testing carried out with third-party labs including TÜV and Intertek.

Where is the equipment made?

In our own factory in Qingdao, Shandong, China, with laser cutting, robot welding, an automatic coating line and assembly under one roof.

How do I get a quote?

Add products to your quote list, use the form above or email info@salmonfitness.com. We reply within 24 hours.
